How to buy

Pay by credit card through ECPay when ordering on our website. You can also purchase through 7-ELEVEN MyShip or Shopee. Check the order page and checkout confirmation for prices, shipping fees, dispatch times, and what is included.

Dispatch time

Custom orders ship within 3 days.

Custom orders

Custom products are made to the submitted design. We use the name as entered. Confirm the spelling, typeface and colors before submitting.

If an exception to the right to cancel applies to a custom product, we will disclose it before ordering. The individual order and applicable law will govern.

Returns and refunds

一般通訊交易商品依台灣消費者保護法辦理，原則上可於收受商品後七日內以退回商品或書面通知方式解除契約。If an exception to the right to cancel applies to a custom product, we will disclose it before ordering. The individual order and applicable law will govern.

For defects, damage or an incorrect item, email your order number, a description and photos to customer service. We will check the order and discuss the next steps. For marketplace orders, you may also use that platform’s return or refund process.

Digital files and licences

The licence for each design determines permitted uses, including commercial use. Check the file format, required parts and printing equipment before purchasing.

Google address search

Optional address search sends your query to Google Maps Platform for suggestions. We do not store search history or Google result lists. Orders retain only the delivery address you confirm. You may also enter your address manually. This feature is subject to the Google Maps Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy。
