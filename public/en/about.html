A FEW GOOD ANSWERS

A little more to know.

How do physical goods and digital files differ?

Physical goods are ready to play with. Digital files are for printing and assembling yourself and do not include a physical product.

Are pressing and spinning different play styles?

Yes. Press designs focus on the feel of mechanical switches; spin designs focus on finger-driven rotation. See each listing for structure and parts.

Can I sell prints made from digital files?

Licenses vary by design. Check the specific terms before buying. Do not resell files or use them for commercial production without explicit permission.

Can I buy now?

For products on sale, choose MyShip or Shopee on the product page. Products not yet on sale are marked accordingly.
